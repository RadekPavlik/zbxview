## English (en-US)

### Short description (≤ 80 chars)
`Zabbix monitoring on your phone — problems, dashboards, host maps, TV kiosk.`

### Full description (≤ 4000 chars)
ZbxView is a fast, no-nonsense mobile client for the Zabbix monitoring system. Connect your own Zabbix server and keep an eye on your infrastructure straight from your pocket.

Built on the Zabbix API — read-only, plus acknowledging and closing events. You can't accidentally break anything in production.

KEY FEATURES
• Real-time problems — a live overview of events, colour-coded by severity just like Zabbix web.
• Acknowledge & close — handle events straight from your phone, with a comment.
• Host geomap — your hosts on a map with zoom-based clustering; works offline too.
• Dashboards — your Zabbix dashboards and widgets on phone or tablet.
• TV / kiosk mode — full-screen dashboard with the screen kept awake, ideal for a wall panel.
• Multiple servers — switch between several Zabbix instances with one tap.
• Sync across devices — carry your setup between phone and tablet.
• Home-screen widget — open-problem count right on your home screen.
• Share a problem — send an event to Teams, WhatsApp or e-mail.
• Dark mode — easy on the eyes during night shifts.
• Enterprise-friendly — deploy via MDM (Microsoft Intune) with managed configuration.

FREE & PRO
ZbxView is free and supported by a small banner ad. A one-time "Pro" purchase removes ads.

PRIVACY
The app connects only to the Zabbix servers you configure. Your credentials and settings stay on your device — nothing is sent to the developer.

Privacy policy: https://radekpavlik.github.io/zbxview/privacy.html

ZbxView is an independent client and is not affiliated with, endorsed by or sponsored by Zabbix SIA. "Zabbix" is a trademark of Zabbix SIA.
